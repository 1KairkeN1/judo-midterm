Dojo Pillars & Philosophy

Seiryoku Zenyo

Maximum efficiency with minimum effort. Focus on technique, leverage, and momentum rather than raw brute force.

Jita Kyoei

Mutual welfare and benefit. Training partners work together to improve each other's physical and mental skills.

Reigi & Respect

Respect for instructors, opponents, and the dojo mat is central to safety and personal growth in martial arts.

Training Programs

Beginner

Learn fundamental breakfalls (Ukemi) and basic standing throws in a safe, structured environment.

Intermediate

Full-body cardiovascular conditioning combined with practical ground control, joint locks, and throw defenses.

Olympic

Intensive Randori sparring, strategic combination attacks, and physical preparation for Olympics.
